tikz-cd

KaTeX's CD only does square grids. For diagonals, bends and dashes, put tikz-cd source in a text/tikzcd script. Arrow options: direction (u d l r), "label" (add ' for the other side, description to sit on the arrow), no head, dashed, dotted, two heads, maps to, bend left/bend right[=deg]. Spacing: data-row-sep, data-col-sep (em).

<script type="text/tikzcd">
C \arrow[dr, dashed, "\exists!\Phi" description] \arrow[ddr, bend right, "\varphi_A"'] \arrow[drr, bend left, "\varphi_B"] & & \\
& A \oplus B \arrow[d, "p_A"] \arrow[r, "p_B"'] & B \\
& A &
</script>

Preamble

Page-specific macros go in a text/tex-preamble script (anywhere in the page). Use \def or \newcommand; \N \Z \Q \R \C are already defined everywhere.

<script type="text/tex-preamble">
\def\im{\operatorname{im}}
\def\set#1{\{#1\}}
</script>
