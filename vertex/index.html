Recoil is real. The shotgun throws you 8 m/s away from wherever it points. Down is up. Backwards is forwards. Two shells; they reload while you touch the ground.

Momentum charges (two at most) come from killing things while airborne, from weak-point kills and from target sequences. A charge buys a dash, or a second kick off the same wall.

Carve. Turning in the air with a direction held adds speed. Jump again the instant you land and none of it is lost.

Pistol trips anchors, which pull you. Rail bounces off mirrors and trips white switches.
